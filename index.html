What I do

Institutional payments products are hard to sell because they are hard to explain. Clearing, settlement and liquidity mechanics are unfamiliar to most of the people who ultimately approve them, and the material that explains those mechanics is usually written for engineers.

My work sits in that gap. I take technical product realities and turn them into narratives, assets and programmes that commercial teams can use in front of banks, partners and enterprise buyers — then make sure those narratives hold up under scrutiny from product, legal and compliance.

Before financial infrastructure I ran regional brand campaigns for a retail investment platform across APAC, and owned marketing end to end at an early-stage fintech. I also build software, which means I can hold a real conversation with a product team about what we are actually selling.

How I work

  • Start from the decision, not the product. Work backwards from what the person has to approve, and what they need to believe in order to approve it.
  • Accuracy is a marketing constraint, not an obstacle. In regulated and institutional contexts, a claim that cannot survive legal review is worthless regardless of how well it converts.
  • Build systems, not one-off assets. A narrative other people can adapt without me is worth more than a perfect deck only I can deliver.
  • Say what is actually known. If a number has no baseline or the attribution is loose, I would rather describe what was observed than dress it up.
